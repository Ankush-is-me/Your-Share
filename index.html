Terms & Conditions

Please read and accept the terms before using Your Share.

1. About Your Share
Your Share helps users divide a merchant bill and create individual UPI payment links and QR codes for each share. It is not a wallet or a bank and does not hold money.

2. Payments
Your Share does not request, collect, or store UPI PINs, OTPs, bank passwords, card PINs, or other payment credentials. Your Share does not process payments; they are made in your own UPI/payment app.

3. Verify payment information
You are responsible for verifying the payee name, payment destination and amount in your payment app before confirming any transaction. Decoding a QR does not mean Your Share has verified the payee.

4. No payment status
Your Share cannot independently verify whether a payment succeeded. Check your UPI/payment app for the transaction result.

5. User responsibility
You must not use the service for fraudulent, deceptive, unauthorized, or unlawful transactions.

6. Privacy
QR data is processed in your browser and is not sent to a server. Only your theme, terms acceptance and cookie choice are stored locally on your device.
